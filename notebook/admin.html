How these numbers are made: travel times come from the AI model trained on TomTom data. Exit counts, carpools and participation are calculated from the resident list (500 fictional residents plus anyone who signs up), using the leave time, how much earlier they can leave, and the school run of every resident. The "after" bars move willing residents into earlier 5-minute slots so each exit stays under capacity. Merchant rewards are demo figures. A one-term pilot replaces the fictional residents with real participants.
